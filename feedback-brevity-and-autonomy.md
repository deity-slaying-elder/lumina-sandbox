Lead with the answer in a few lines. James asked for a shorter version of an answer or draft
at least eight times in one session ("tldr as always", "no need to explain so much",
"Huh bro TLDR please"). Once he has decided, he does not want more brainstorming or
option-listing: "can you please worked on it already... go be autonomous please".

**Why:** he reads the first screenful and acts on it; long explanations cost him a round trip
to ask for the short version. Re-litigating a settled decision reads as stalling.

**How to apply:** default to a TLDR block first, detail below only when it changes what he
does. When he says a client has decided, stop presenting options and build, stating
assumptions inline. Still label confidence on technical claims (he challenges "are you sure"
often and was right to). Outbound drafts: see [[feedback_user_writing_voice]] and the
`drafting-stakeholder-messages` skill (no em-dashes, plain, short).
