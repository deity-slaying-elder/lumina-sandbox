Harness review · window 2026-06-01 → 2026-09-01 · 2,111 sessions across 51 project folders · all clients

The honest answer to "is plan mode better than what I've been doing": the corpus cannot tell you yet, and anyone who says otherwise is guessing.

Plan mode adoption over three months is zero. Not low — zero. There is exactly one genuine ExitPlanMode call in the entire 3,747-file archive, and it is today's trial. There is no plan-mode bucket to compare against, so no before/after claim is available.

What the data does show is that the thing actually costing you is not planning discipline. It is session length and inline exploration. Twenty sessions carry 53% of all turns, and 194 of 217 heavy-exploration sessions dispatched nothing to a subagent.

The negative control matters here. Median session-start context is 34.4K (p90 50.6K). That is small. Trimming CLAUDE.md, skills or plugin descriptions cannot move your bill. The lever is session length, not prompt size. Do not let anyone sell you a prompt-trimming fix.

6. So: should you use plan mode?

Not provable from your data. But the reasoning that survives the absence of data:

  • Where it plausibly helps you: the M&D Capital build sessions where rework runs 88% and deploy-retry 80%. A read-only recon pass before the first edit is cheap to throw away; a wrong 200-turn diff is not.
  • Where it will not help: the 4,499-turn / 6,283-bash session. That is a termination problem. A plan at the top does not stop a loop in the middle.
  • What it costs: close to nothing. Questions inside plan mode are free — it is read-only by construction, so there is no partial state to corrupt and nothing to unwind.

Your own instinct on 2026-09-01 was right, and it matches where the rework actually is. The measurable claim to test is narrow: does entering plan mode before a Salesforce build session reduce rework edits per 100 turns below the 8.98 baseline? Ten plan-mode build sessions makes that answerable. Right now it is not.

The bigger lever, stated plainly. If you change one habit this month, make it ending sessions rather than adopting plan mode. Twenty sessions carrying 53% of your turns is a far larger effect than anything plan mode could plausibly buy, and it is entirely within your control — /clear at a natural boundary, or a fresh session per ticket.
